Inspira UI adaptations in this directory:
- InspiraAurora.vue:      app/components/inspira/ui/aurora-background/AuroraBackground.vue
- InspiraBorderBeam.vue:  app/components/inspira/ui/border-beam/BorderBeam.vue
- InspiraShimmer.vue:     app/components/inspira/ui/shimmer-button/ShimmerButton.vue
- InspiraNumberTicker.vue: app/components/inspira/ui/number-ticker/NumberTicker.vue
- InspiraMagicCard.vue:   app/components/inspira/ui/card-spotlight/CardSpotlight.vue
                          (Inspira UI's Vue port of Magic UI's MagicCard; unovue/inspira-ui ships no
                          MagicCard.vue, the cursor-following gradient card is CardSpotlight.vue)
                          cross-reference: magicuidesign/magicui apps/www/registry/magicui/magic-card.tsx
